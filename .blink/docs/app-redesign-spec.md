Scope, design direction and decisions for the minimalist-ui app surface: warm
stone base, flat muted-indigo accent, Geist + Geist Mono + a serif for editorial
headings, hairline borders, dark mode first-class. The later editorial-shell
redesign (dissolved sidebar, no top bar, Newsreader serif, centered 1240px
frame) is a plan on top of it, not a replacement.
