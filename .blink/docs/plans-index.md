`docs/superpowers/plans/` holds 28 executed slice plans and
`docs/superpowers/specs/` holds 12 design specs, dated `YYYY-MM-DD-<name>.md`.
They are the written record of how [[m-01]] was built, one file per slice, and
the latest of them is the pattern to copy when planning new work.

They are deliberately **not** converted into tracker entities: they describe
work that is finished, and 40 doc files would bury the handful of live
references above. Read them by date; `progress.md` says which slice each one
belongs to.
